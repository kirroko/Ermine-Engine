MACHINA by Ermine Works
A first-person steampunk puzzle-platformer built with the Ermine Engine.

Sacrifice your life essence to power a reality-bending weapon. Shoot orbs of energy, teleport through deadly chambers, blind hostile lights and constructs, and escape a mysterious facility before your life runs out.

------------------------------------------------------------
GAME OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------

You awaken in an experimental steampunk facility with a strange weapon bound to your life force. Every ability you use drains your health (life essence), but in return you gain powerful skills:

- Shoot a traveling essence orb
- Teleport to the orb’s position
- Release a blinding burst that disrupts lights and enemies
- Recall the orb to reset your position and plan your next move

Your goal is to navigate lethal light-filled chambers, solve environmental puzzles, and reach the exit before your life essence is completely consumed.

------------------------------------------------------------
CORE MECHANICS
------------------------------------------------------------

LIGHT IS LETHAL
- Direct light is dangerous. Staying in exposed, brightly lit areas will rapidly drain your health.
- Stay in shadows, behind cover, or disable lights to survive.

LIFE ESSENCE SKILL SYSTEM
Each skill drains health and has its own cooldown:

- Skill Slot 0 – Shoot Essence Orb
  - Input: First Left Click (Mouse 1)
  - Fires an orb of life essence forward, creating a teleport anchor.

- Skill Slot 1 – Teleport to Orb
  - Input: Second Left Click (Mouse 1) while an orb is active
  - Instantly teleports you to the orb’s location and consumes the orb.

- Skill Slot 2 – Blind Burst
  - Input: Right Click (Mouse 2)
  - Releases a burst that blinds/weakens nearby lights and enemies, opening safe paths in otherwise deadly spaces.

- Skill Slot 3 – Recall Orb
  - Input: R key
  - Recalls/destroys the active orb so you can reposition and cast a new one.

HEALTH REGENERATION
- Regeneration Rate: 5 HP per second.
- Delay: Regeneration starts 3 seconds after your last skill use.
- Condition: Health only regenerates when no skills are on cooldown (idle state).
- Playstyle: Use abilities deliberately, then retreat to safety and wait for regeneration before engaging again.

ENVIRONMENTAL PUZZLES & PLATFORMS
- Navigate moving and rotating platforms, vertical and upwards lifts.
- Use switches, doors, keys, and elevators to progress.
- Avoid death pits and void zones that instantly kill the player.
- Enemies use simple state-based AI (idle, patrol, chase, attack) to hunt you down in lighted areas.

------------------------------------------------------------
USER CONTROLS
------------------------------------------------------------

MOVEMENT
- WASD          - Move the player
- Space         - Jump

CORE SKILLS (LIFE ESSENCE WEAPON)
- Left Click    - First click: Shoot Essence Orb
                - Second click (while orb is active): Teleport to the Orb
- Right Click   - Blind Burst (disable / weaken enemies and lights)
- R             - Recall Orb (destroy the active orb and reset anchor)
- E             - Interact with objects (switches, doors, etc.)

GENERAL TIPS
- Plan your path before stepping into the light.
- Use the orb to scout and teleport across large gaps and dangerous light zones.
- Blind Burst is expensive—use it to break through heavily lit choke points.
- Always leave time for your life essence to regenerate before pushing deeper into the facility.
